## EN 正文（可直接发布）
**7 Inspection Gates: From Drawing to Vacuum Chamber Delivery**

1. **Drawing review & DFM** — every drawing is reviewed before fabrication; manufacturability, tolerances and leak-path risks are flagged and agreed with the customer.
2. **Incoming material inspection** — material certificates and surface condition are checked against the specification before stock release.
3. **Blanking & bevel preparation** — thick-plate cutting on a 30,000 W laser; weld bevels prepared in the same pass for consistent fit-up.
4. **Precision machining** — CNC and H200 floor-type boring-milling with 4 m-class rotation; key dimensions are recorded per part.
5. **Welding** — TIG and automated processes across 40+ gas-shielded stations in a controlled clean welding environment; weld records kept per chamber.
6. **Helium leak testing** — UL1000-class mass-spectrometer leak test to the 1×10⁻⁹ Pa·m³/s order; every chamber verified, not sampled.
7. **Final inspection & delivery** — dimensional and surface checks against the drawing, with inspection reports shipped alongside the unit.

## ZH 正文（可直接发布）
**从图纸到交付：真空腔体的 7 道检验**

1. 图纸评审与 DFM——制造前逐图评审，可制造性、公差与泄漏路径风险先与客户对齐。
2. 来料检验——按规格核对材料质保书与表面状态后放行入库。
3. 下料与坡口——3 万瓦激光厚板切割，坡口同工序一次成形，装配间隙稳定。
4. 精密加工——CNC 与 H200 落地镗铣（4 m 级回转），关键尺寸逐件记录。
5. 焊接——40+ 气保焊工位，TIG 与自动化工艺，受控洁净焊接环境，焊缝记录随腔体留存。
6. 氦质谱检漏——UL1000 级、1×10⁻⁹ Pa·m³/s 量级，逐台验证而非抽检。
7. 出厂检验与交付——按图纸做尺寸与表面终检，检验报告随货交付。

给设备团队的价值：可追溯、有记录、按图纸交付——不是口头承诺。发来图纸，同一套检验流程作用于您的腔体。
